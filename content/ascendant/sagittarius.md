## Source verse

> The person with Sagittarius Ascendant at birth — His face and neck will be long and his nose and ears will be big. He will always keep himself engaged in some work or the other. He will be eloquent in speech and self sacrificing. He will have a short stature. He will be courageous and will overcome his enemies. He will be wealthy and favourite of the king. He can be won over by persuation and kindness.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Sagittarius ascendant as a placement of philosophy, dharma, and expansive vision. The lagna lord is Jupiter; teaching, religious inclination, and ethical clarity are central themes. Classical authority describes a sturdy body, a generous nature, and a mind that travels in ideas as readily as in places. Wisdom and standing follow when Jupiter is well-placed; over-extension and restless idealism appear when weak.
