## Source verse

> Person with Cancer as the Ascendant will be henpacked. He will have a thick neck and will be fond of making friendships. He will possess many houses and will be wealthy. His waist will be thick but he will be short statured. He will be intelligent and fond of watery sports like swimming etc. He will walk fast and will have few sons. He will be crooked.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Cancer ascendant as a placement of emotion, family, and inner sensitivity. The lagna lord is the Moon; the home, mother, and emotional life are central themes. Classical authority describes a softer constitution, susceptible to mood and to the lunar phase. Stability and renown follow when the Moon is waxing and well-placed; weak Moon brings hesitancy and emotional turbulence.
