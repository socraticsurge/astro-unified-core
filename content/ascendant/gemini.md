## Source verse

> When the Ascendant is Gemini, the native will have black eyes and curly hair. He will enjoy association with women and will have thick neck and many friends. He will be intelligent and mind reader. His nose will be elevated and he will be fond of music and dancing. He will like to remain indoors.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Gemini ascendant as a placement of intellect, communication, and adaptability. The lagna lord is Mercury; learning, speech, and short journeys characterise the life. Classical authority describes a body of medium build and a temperament inclined toward humour, curiosity, and many concerns at once. Sustained effort can be a challenge; renown follows when Mercury is well-placed and supported by Jupiter.
