## Source verse

> The person with Scorpio Ascendant at birth, will have rounded thighs and knees. His chest will be broad and eyes expansive. He will suffer from diseases from an early age. He will beseperated from his parents and preceptor. He will indulge In cruel actions and will be honoured by the king. His feet will bear marks of lotus.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Scorpio ascendant as a placement of intensity, depth, and transformation. The lagna lord is Mars; the temperament holds reservoirs of will and is drawn to what lies beneath the surface. Classical authority describes a strong but secretive constitution, capable of profound concentration and equally profound resistance. Power follows when Mars is well-placed; obsession and withdrawal emerge when afflicted.
