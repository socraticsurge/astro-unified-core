## Source verse

> When Capricorn is the Ascendant at birth, his lower limbs will be weak. He will have vitality. He will accept other people's advice but will be lazy by nature. He will indulge in affairs with old women of the forbidden category. He will be a religious hypocrite. He will be fond of wandering. He will be fortunate but shameless. He will suffer from wind-diseases.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Capricorn ascendant as a placement of structure, persistence, and slow accumulation. The lagna lord is Saturn; discipline, hierarchy, and earned authority characterise the life. Classical authority describes a leaner constitution, a serious temperament, and capacity for sustained labour. Recognition arrives later in life and is earned; difficulty and constraint mark the early years if Saturn is afflicted.
