## Source verse

> If the Ascendant be Libra, the person born will rever gods, and Brahmins but he will be active and will possess a lean and thin body. He will be tall in stature, deft in trading, patient and intent on being first. He will often have two names. He will have few children and he will be fond of wandering. He will be brave, merciless and impartial In his arguments.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Libra ascendant as a placement of balance, partnership, and aesthetic sensibility. The lagna lord is Venus; harmony in relationships and pleasure in the arts are central themes. Classical authority describes a graceful body and a mind inclined toward justice and refinement. Marriage and partnership are emphasised; indecision and dependence on others appear when Venus is afflicted.
