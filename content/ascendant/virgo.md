## Source verse

> The person born with Virgo as the Ascendant, will be truthful and will speak kindly. His eyes will be full of bashfulness and will be immediately liked by others. He will be well versed in Shastras and will take advantage of other people's houses and wealth. His arms and shoulders will be drooped and will possess few sons.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Virgo ascendant as a placement of discrimination, service, and detailed work. The lagna lord is Mercury; the temperament tilts toward analysis, refinement, and practical capability. Classical authority describes a body of medium stature and a mind inclined to questioning and learning. Effort tends to find its reward when Mercury is well-placed; over-thinking and self-criticism appear when afflicted.
