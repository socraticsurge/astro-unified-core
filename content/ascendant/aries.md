## Source verse

> If the Ascendant falls in Aries, the native will have round eyes. His knees will be weak and he will have aggressive temperament but he will be afraid of water. He will be active and will always be moving about. His limbs will be bruised. He will eat sparigly and will be passionate. He will speak falsehoods.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Aries ascendant as a placement of strength, will, and physical vigour. The lagna lord is Mars; the chart is built around courage, action, and direct effort. Classical authority describes a body that is well-formed and durable, with energy that can tilt toward impatience or anger if Mars is afflicted. Wealth and standing follow when Mars is well-placed and benefics aspect the lagna.
