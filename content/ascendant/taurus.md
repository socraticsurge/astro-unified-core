## Source verse

> The person born with Taurus as the Ascendant, will have plumply thighs and a big face. He will be engaged in agricultural ventures. He will be happy in the middle and last portions of his life. He will be fond of enjoying young women. He will be of sacrificing and forgiving nature, will be capable of endowing hardships and will possess cattle. He will have marks or moles on the back, face and sides.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Taurus ascendant as a placement of beauty, patience, and material foundation. The lagna lord is Venus; sustained accumulation and family enjoyment are central themes. Classical authority describes a substantial physical presence and a temperament inclined toward comfort, harmony, and sensuality. Wealth follows when Venus is well-placed; obstinacy and slowness emerge when the second house is afflicted.
