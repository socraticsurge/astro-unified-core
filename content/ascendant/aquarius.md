## Source verse

> The person at whose birth Aquarius is the Ascendant, will commit sinful actions secretly. He will have little wealth, will be greedy and eager to usurp or utilise other people's money. He will endure long marches on road and will be clever in causing hurt to others. He will have a body like a water pot. He will be fond of perfumes and flowers. His financial position will be of a fluctuating nature.

— *Phaladeepika (Mantreswara)*. Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Phaladeepika reads the Aquarius ascendant as a placement of intellect, friendship, and detached observation. The lagna lord is Saturn (with Rahu as co-significator in modern reading); the temperament inclines toward the unconventional, the philosophical, and the social. Classical authority describes a tall, lean body and a mind capable of long-range thought. Recognition comes through groups and ideas; alienation appears when Saturn is afflicted.
