## What this section measures

The Bhinnashtakavarga ("split eight-divisions") is the planet-by-planet breakdown that builds up to the Sarvashtakavarga. Each of the seven planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn — has its own eight contributing reference points (the other six planets, the lagna, and the planet itself), each of which marks certain houses from itself as auspicious for that planet. The total bindus contributed by all eight references across the twelve signs is the planet's Bhinnashtakavarga.

The maximum bindus a planet can receive in any single sign is 8 (every reference point marks it auspicious). The minimum is 0. The total bindus distributed across the twelve signs is fixed per planet, varying by planet.

## What the section shows

DashaflowView renders the Bhinnashtakavarga as a 7×12 table — seven rows (one per planet from Sun to Saturn) and twelve columns (one per sign). Each cell shows the bindu count for that planet in that sign. The final column shows the total per planet, summing to the Bhinnashtakavarga total.

A bottom row shows the Sarvashtakavarga — the column-wise total — for cross-reference.

## How individual cells are read

The Bhinnashtakavarga of a single planet in a single sign is a count of how many of the eight reference points "favour" the planet in that sign. The convention DashaflowView uses follows the standard interpretive scale:

| Bindus | Reading |
|---|---|
| 6–8 | Strong placement for that planet |
| 4–5 | Average |
| 1–3 | Weak placement |
| 0 | Very weak — every reference point dis-favours the planet here |

The reading is read together with the planet's dignity, shadbala, and avastha. A planet in its sign of debilitation but with 7 bindus in that sign is partly compensated by the strong Ashtakavarga charge; a planet exalted but with 1 bindu in its sign is structurally undercut.

## Two specific uses

*BPHS* Chapters 67–69 cover three rectifications and refinements applied to the raw Bhinnashtakavarga before the final reading: *Trikona Sodhana* (trinal rectification), *Ekadhipatya Sodhana* (the rectification when one planet rules two signs), and *Pinda Sadhana* (the consolidation). DashaflowView reports the post-rectification values typically used in working interpretation.

The Sun's Bhinnashtakavarga total in the lagna sign and adjacent signs is treated in the longevity literature as a key input. The Moon's Bhinnashtakavarga in the dasha-lord sign is similarly treated as a strength indicator for that period.

## How the section is read

The table reads in two directions. Reading row-by-row gives a per-planet picture: where the planet is favourably "charged" across the zodiac, where it is weakly placed regardless of its actual sign. Reading column-by-column gives a per-sign picture for that planet — useful when assessing transit effects (a transit through a sign with a high Bhinnashtakavarga for that planet is strengthened).

The Sarvashtakavarga row at the bottom of the table is the same data as the Sarvashtakavarga section above; it is repeated here for cross-reference convenience when working from the Bhinnashtakavarga upward.
