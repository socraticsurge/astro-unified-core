## What Are Divisional Charts?

The *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* teaches that the zodiac is not a single map but a **nested system of overlapping lenses**. Each zodiac sign (30°) can be divided into smaller sub-divisions, and each sub-division is mapped back onto the 12 signs of the zodiac to form an entirely new chart. Parasara describes this as the *Shodashavarga* — the sixteen divisional charts that together reveal the full texture of a soul's karma.

> *"As a father distributes different tasks among his children according to their nature, so does the Almighty distribute different portions of the zodiac to examine different departments of life."*  
> — Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (paraphrase)

## The Critical Role of D9 (Navamsa)

The Navamsa is often called the **second birth chart**. Parasara instructs that no prediction from the D1 chart should be confirmed unless the D9 chart supports it. A planet that is weak or debilitated in D1 but exalted or in its own sign in D9 is said to *"mature into strength"* after the age of 36. Conversely, a strong D1 planet with a weak D9 placement may not fully deliver its promise.

## The D10 (Dashamsha) and Career

The 10th divisional chart is the dedicated map of **career and public life**. B.V. Raman wrote that the D10 chart should be studied like a full birth chart for all matters related to profession. Look especially for:
- The D10 Lagna lord's placement and strength.
- The 10th lord in D10 and its conjunctions.
- The planet most strongly placed in D10, which will reveal the natural career domain.
