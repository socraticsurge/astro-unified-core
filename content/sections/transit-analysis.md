## What Is Transit Analysis?

While the birth chart (*Rasi chart*) is a fixed snapshot of the sky at the moment of your birth, the planets continue to move through the zodiac throughout your life. This ongoing motion is called *Gochar* (transit) in Vedic astrology. Parasara teaches that **transits activate the promises already embedded in the natal chart** — they do not create new fate, but they are the key that unlocks dormant karmas at the right time.

> *"Just as the same seed yields different fruit depending on season and soil, the natal planets yield their results through the agency of transits."*  
> — BPHS (paraphrase), Ch. 85

## How to Read This Section

The transit view shows, for each planet:
- Its **current sign** in the sky (the transit position).
- Its **natal house** — where it lived at birth.
- Its **transit house** — where it is moving through now, counted from the natal Moon (the primary reference point in Vedic transit analysis).
- Whether its transit is considered **auspicious or inauspicious** from the natal Moon's position.

## Saturn Transits and Sade Sati

**Sade Sati** (*seven-and-a-half years*) is one of the most discussed Saturn transit patterns in Vedic astrology. It occurs when Saturn transits through:
1. The 12th sign from the natal Moon (*preparation phase*),
2. The sign of the natal Moon itself (*peak phase*),
3. The 2nd sign from the natal Moon (*resolution phase*).

Each phase lasts approximately **2.5 years**. The effects depend heavily on Saturn's natal dignity and the Moon's natal condition. Sade Sati is not uniformly negative — for those with a strong Saturn or a Moon in Capricorn/Aquarius, it can be a period of disciplined achievement.

## The Rahu-Ketu Axis

Rahu and Ketu transit in **retrograde motion**, spending approximately **18 months in each sign**. Their transit over or opposite natal planets activates karmic nodes of experience — sudden events, unusual opportunities, foreign connections (Rahu), or spiritual withdrawals and releases (Ketu).

## Transit Ashtakavarga (SAV Points)

Each sign in the chart carries a *bindu* (point) score based on contributions from all 7 planets plus the Lagna. When a transiting planet passes through a sign with **high SAV points (≥ 4)**, its results tend to be more pronounced and often beneficial. Signs with **low SAV (≤ 2)** tend to mute or obstruct the transiting planet's results.
