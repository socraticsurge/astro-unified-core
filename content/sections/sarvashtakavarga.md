## What this section measures

Ashtakavarga is Parasara's quantitative system for measuring how each sign in a chart has been "charged" with auspiciousness by the planets. *BPHS* Chapter 66 introduces it as the framework Parasara devised specifically for the common reader who cannot navigate the contradictions of competing classical effects:

> "There will be no contradictions in judging the effects of happiness and sorrows and for determination of the longevity. You now listen to me carefully."
> — BPHS 66.5–6

The system assigns each of seven planets (Sun through Saturn — Rahu and Ketu are not assigned bindus in the standard Ashtakavarga) a *bindu* (point) in each sign based on whether that sign is auspicious from the planet's natural reference points. Adding the bindus from all seven planets in each sign produces the *Sarvashtakavarga*, the "all-eight-divisions" combined total.

## How the SAV is computed

For each planet, eight reference points contribute bindus: the seven other planets plus the lagna. From each reference point, certain houses are read as auspicious for the planet and contribute one *bindu* each. After totalling for one planet, the result is the *Bhinnashtakavarga* of that planet — its individual sign-by-sign profile (rendered separately in the next section).

Adding the seven Bhinnashtakavargas together gives the *Sarvashtakavarga*: the sign-by-sign total of all seven. This is rendered as a single row of twelve numbers across the signs from Aries to Pisces.

The total points distributed across all twelve signs is fixed at 337. The average per sign is therefore approximately 28. *BPHS* Chapter 70 covers the detailed effects of the SAV total in each sign; the convention DashaflowView uses is the standard interpretive threshold:

| SAV total | Reading |
|---|---|
| ≥ 30 | Strong sign |
| 28–29 | Average |
| 22–27 | Below average |
| < 22 | Weak sign |

## How the section is read

The Sarvashtakavarga is read sign-by-sign: a sign with a high SAV is "charged" — events related to the houses that sign rules tend to unfold favourably; a sign with a low SAV indicates a structurally weak area regardless of which planet sits there. The colour coding makes this immediate: emerald green for ≥28 (strong), red for <22 (weak), neutral otherwise.

The reading is composite with the chart's other layers. A sign with high SAV is doubly auspicious if it also contains a strong well-dignified planet; a sign with low SAV is doubly difficult if it contains a debilitated or afflicted planet. SAV does not replace the readings of dignity, shadbala, or yoga — it qualifies them.

For predictive use, the standard convention is to read the SAV of the sign where the dasha lord sits and the SAV of the houses the dasha lord rules. Periods unfold more smoothly when the dasha lord operates from or rules signs with a high SAV total.
