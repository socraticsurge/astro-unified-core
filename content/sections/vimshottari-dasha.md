## What this section measures

The Vimshottari Dasha is Parasara's principal scheme for timing the unfolding of a life. *Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra* Chapter 46 introduces it as the most appropriate of the various dasha systems for the general populace:

> "Dasas (periods) are of many kinds. Amongst them Vimshottari is the most appropriate for the general populace."
> — BPHS 46.2

The system divides every lifetime into nine periods ruled in sequence by the nine planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus. The lengths of the periods are fixed, total 120 years, and play out once per lifetime. The starting point is determined by the moon's nakshatra at birth.

## The nine planets and their periods

Parasara fixes the period of each planet in *BPHS* 46.15:

> "The periods of Dasas of the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu and Venus are 6, 10, 7, 18, 16, 19, 17, 7 and 20 in that order."
> — BPHS 46.15

Sum: 6 + 10 + 7 + 18 + 16 + 19 + 17 + 7 + 20 = 120 years. The full cycle plays out once in a lifetime, in this fixed order. Once the cycle reaches Venus, it returns to Ketu and continues — a person who lives past 120 begins a second iteration.

## How the starting point is set

The moon's nakshatra at birth determines which planet's mahadasha you begin under, and how much of that mahadasha was already past at the moment of birth. *BPHS* 46.12–14 maps each nakshatra to its dasha lord:

| Nakshatras | Dasa Lord | Years |
|---|---|---|
| Krittika, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashada | Sun | 6 |
| Rohini, Hasta, Sravana | Moon | 10 |
| Mrigasira, Chitra, Dhanishta | Mars | 7 |
| Ardra, Swati, Satabhisha | Rahu | 18 |
| Punarvasu, Vishakha, Purva Bhadrapada | Jupiter | 16 |
| Pushya, Anuradha, Uttara Bhadrapada | Saturn | 19 |
| Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati | Mercury | 17 |
| Magha, Moola, Ashwini | Ketu | 7 |
| Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashada, Bharani | Venus | 20 |

The 27 nakshatras are grouped into nine sets of three, and the lord of the moon's nakshatra rules the dasha you are born into. This is one of two reasons (the other is the panchang's emphasis on it) that the moon's nakshatra is the most consequential single value in the chart's timing layer.

## The five levels

Each mahadasha contains nested sub-periods:

| Level | Duration scale | Ruled by |
|---|---|---|
| Mahadasha | 6 to 20 years | One ruling planet |
| Antardasha | months to years | Sub-period within mahadasha, ruled by a second planet |
| Pratyantardasha | weeks to months | Sub-sub-period, ruled by a third planet |
| Sukshma | days to weeks | Ruled by a fourth planet |
| Prana | hours to days | Ruled by a fifth planet |

Each level is ruled by one of the same nine planets, in a sequence determined by the level above. The reading is composite: the Prana shades the Sukshma, which shades the Pratyantar, which shades the Antar, which shades the Maha. *BPHS* devotes Chapters 47–64 to the effects of antardashas within each mahadasha — eighteen chapters of detailed coverage, indicating the centrality of the system in Parasara's framework.
