## The Nested Structure of Vimshottari Dasha

Parasara's *Vimshottari Dasha* system unfolds over 120 years through **nine planetary lords** in a fixed sequence: Sun (6 yrs), Moon (10 yrs), Mars (7 yrs), Rahu (18 yrs), Jupiter (16 yrs), Saturn (19 yrs), Mercury (17 yrs), Ketu (7 yrs), Venus (20 yrs). The starting point is determined by the Moon's nakshatra at birth.

> *"The Mahadasha lord is like a king who sets the tone of an era. The Antardasha lord is the minister who administers day-to-day affairs within that era."*  
> — Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (paraphrase), Ch. 46

Each Mahadasha is further divided into **nine Antardasha sub-periods** ruled by the same nine planets, in the same sequence starting from the Mahadasha lord itself. The duration of each Antardasha is proportional to the Antardasha lord's total Mahadasha allocation.

## Formula for Antardasha Duration

If a planet A has a Mahadasha of **M years** and planet B has a Mahadasha of **B years**, then the Antardasha of B within A's Mahadasha lasts:

> **(M × B) / 120 years**

For example: Within a 16-year Jupiter Mahadasha, Saturn's Antardasha = (16 × 19) / 120 = **2 years, 6 months, 12 days**.

## The Interplay of Lords

The results of an Antardasha period are shaped by the **relationship between the Mahadasha and Antardasha lords**:

| Relationship | Likely Outcome |
|---|---|
| Friends (e.g., Jupiter-Sun) | Generally auspicious; cooperative energy |
| Neutrals | Mixed results; neither clearly beneficial nor harmful |
| Enemies (e.g., Sun-Saturn) | Tension, setbacks, health concerns |
| Mutual exchange (Parivartana) | Powerful activation of both planets' houses |

Always cross-reference the period lords' natal placements, dignities, and house rulerships to arrive at a precise reading.
