## What this section measures

*Gandanta* — literally "the end of a knot" — refers to planets sitting in the narrow degree-bands at the junctions between specific signs. The bands are at the transitions from water signs to fire signs in the natural zodiac:

| Junction | Last 0°48' (Gandanta zone of water sign) | First 0°48' (Gandanta zone of fire sign) |
|---|---|---|
| Cancer to Leo | 29°12'–30° Cancer | 0°–0°48' Leo |
| Scorpio to Sagittarius | 29°12'–30° Scorpio | 0°–0°48' Sagittarius |
| Pisces to Aries | 29°12'–30° Pisces | 0°–0°48' Aries |

These three junctions correspond to the boundaries between three pairs of nakshatras: Ashlesha to Magha, Jyeshtha to Moola, and Revati to Ashwini. The "Gandanta degrees" are the last pada of the water-sign nakshatra and the first pada of the fire-sign nakshatra.

The Moon in *Gandanta* is treated as the most consequential of the *Gandanta* placements — sufficiently consequential that *BPHS* Chapter 9 (Evils at Birth) discusses *Gandanta* alongside other natal evils requiring remedy, and Chapter 92 prescribes specific remedies for births in *Gandanta*.

## What the section shows

DashaflowView lists each planet in *Gandanta* with the sign and degree of the placement. The section renders only when a *Gandanta* placement is present — a chart with no planet in the *Gandanta* zones shows no entries.

Each entry includes the planet, the sign, the exact degree, and a note on whether the placement is in the water-sign tail (the "ending" *Gandanta*) or the fire-sign head (the "beginning" *Gandanta*).

## How the section is read

The *Gandanta* reading is structurally a degree-based reading: a planet in *Gandanta* is treated as karmically sensitive, the placement representing a transition or "knot" the chart-holder is born into. The reading varies by which planet is in *Gandanta*:

| Planet | Classical reading |
|---|---|
| Moon | The most consequential — the chart-holder's emotional and timing layer (the Moon's nakshatra rules the dasha) is born at a transition point. Treated in *BPHS* Ch. 92 as requiring specific remedy. |
| Sun | The chart-holder's identity and authority sit at a transition; sometimes read as ambiguous social position. |
| Lagna | The body and orientation of the entire chart sit at a junction; treated as the most sensitive *Gandanta* of all when present. |
| Other planets | The significations of that planet are subject to the same transitional reading. |

The reading is mitigated by the rest of the chart — strong shadbala, favourable aspects, and supporting yogas reduce the karmic intensity of *Gandanta*. *BPHS* Chapter 92 describes specific remedial practices (mantras, charitable acts) traditionally prescribed for *Gandanta* births, particularly Moon-Gandanta births.

## A note on calculation conventions

The 0°48' zone width is the standard convention. Some lineages use slightly different widths (e.g., 0°00' to 1°00', or the last/first quarter of the bordering nakshatras). The convention used here is the standard 0°48' (or, equivalently, the last pada of the ending nakshatra and the first pada of the beginning nakshatra). For a planet sitting at, say, 29°45' Pisces, the *Gandanta* reading applies; at 28°30' Pisces, it does not.

The section renders only positive findings. If the chart has no *Gandanta* placements, the section is omitted from the rendered output.
