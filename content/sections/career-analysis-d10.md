## What Is the Dashamsha (D10)?

The 10th divisional chart divides each zodiac sign into **10 equal parts of 3° each**. These ten parts are distributed among the signs in a specific sequence rooted in the nature of the original sign (odd or even). The result is a new chart — the Dashamsha — that functions as a **dedicated map of karma-in-the-world**: your public life, professional achievements, authority, and the kind of work that fulfils your dharma.

> *"By examining the Dashamsha, one may determine the nature of a person's profession, the extent of their worldly success, and whether they will rise to a position of authority or remain in service to others."*  
> — Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (paraphrase), Ch. 7

## Key Factors in the D10 Chart

### 1. The D10 Lagna
The D10 Ascendant shows the **innate orientation toward career**. Its lord's placement in D10 reveals whether professional success comes through one's own initiative, through partnerships, through institutional support, or through struggle.

### 2. The 10th Lord in D10
The lord of the 10th house in D10 is the primary career significator. A strong, well-placed 10th lord in D10 points to consistent professional recognition. An afflicted lord suggests frequent career disruptions or a need for course correction.

### 3. The Sun in D10
The Sun represents authority, government, and the public self. Its strength and placement in D10 shows the native's potential for leadership roles and their relationship with figures of authority.

### 4. Saturn in D10
Saturn is the *karaka* (natural significator) of work, effort, and mass employment. Its placement in D10 reveals the native's work ethic, the kind of work they are drawn to (technical, spiritual, labour-intensive), and whether their career is built slowly through discipline or blocked by karma.
