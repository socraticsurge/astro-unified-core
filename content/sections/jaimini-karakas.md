## What this section measures

The Jaimini Karakas are seven significators (*karakas*) assigned to the seven non-nodal planets in every chart based on the degree each planet occupies in its sign. The system originates in the Jaimini Sutras and is treated alongside Parasara's own *karakatwa* scheme in *BPHS* Chapter 32. Where Parasara assigns each planet a fixed natural significatorship that holds for every chart (Sun signifies father, Moon signifies mother, and so on), the Jaimini system assigns chart-specific karakas based on actual placement.

DashaflowView uses the Jaimini scheme. Each chart produces its own Atmakaraka, its own Amatyakaraka, and so on through the seven roles.

## How the karakas are computed

The seven non-nodal planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn — are ranked by the degree each occupies within its sign. The planet at the highest degree is the Atmakaraka; the next, Amatyakaraka; and so on through seven roles. Some Jaimini lineages include Rahu in the ranking (using 30° minus Rahu's degree, since Rahu is naturally retrograde); others exclude the nodes entirely. DashaflowView follows the seven-planet convention.

The seven roles, in descending order of degree:

| Karaka | Role | Significations |
|---|---|---|
| Atmakaraka | Soul-significator | The orientation of the soul; the chart's most consequential planet |
| Amatyakaraka | Minister | Career, the planet that "advises" the soul |
| Bhratrikaraka | Sibling-significator | Brothers, sisters, courage |
| Matrikaraka | Mother-significator | Mother, home, emotional life |
| Putrakaraka | Children-significator | Progeny, intellect |
| Gnatikaraka | Cousin-significator | Cousins, illness, debts (the dustana significations) |
| Darakaraka | Spouse-significator | Marriage partner, partnerships |

## What each row shows

DashaflowView surfaces the following per karaka:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Karaka | The role (Atmakaraka, Amatyakaraka, etc.) |
| Planet | The planet currently holding that role for this chart |
| Sign | The sign the planet occupies in the rasi chart |
| House | The bhava in the rasi chart |
| D9 sign | The sign the planet occupies in the Navamsa chart |
| Degree | The exact degree in its sign — the basis of the ranking |
| Description | Short note on the karaka's classical reading |

## How the karakas are read

The Atmakaraka is read as the most consequential single planet in the chart — the planet whose lessons the soul has come into this birth to learn. Its placement, dignity, condition, and aspects are read with greater interpretive weight than any other single planet. The Atmakaraka's placement in the Navamsa chart yields the *Karakamsha* (rendered separately), which is read as the soul's spiritual orientation.

The other six karakas qualify the readings of their respective life areas. The Darakaraka, for example, is read alongside the seventh house, the seventh lord, and the Upapada to produce a composite picture of marriage. The Matrikaraka is read alongside the fourth house and the Moon (Parasara's natural significator for mother); a confluence of all three pointing the same direction strengthens the reading; divergence between them invites a more nuanced interpretation.

A note on disagreement between Parasara and Jaimini: where Parasara's natural karakas and Jaimini's chart-specific karakas point in different directions, the working tradition treats both as inputs rather than choosing between them. The natural karakas describe the universal structure; the Jaimini karakas describe how that structure is configured for this particular chart. The reading reconciles both.
