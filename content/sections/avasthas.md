## What this section measures

An *avastha* is the state in which a planet is operating. *BPHS* Chapter 45 sets out three independent schemes of avasthas, which together qualify how strongly and in what mode a planet delivers its results.

DashaflowView renders the *Baladi Avasthas* — the five degree-based states that range from infant to dead. The other avastha schemes are referenced in the chapter but rendered separately in the chart.

## The Baladi Avasthas — five states by degree

Parasara assigns each planet a state based on the degree it occupies within its sign. *BPHS* 45.3–4:

> "Infant, youthful, adolescent, old and dead are the states of planets placed in the ascending order at the rate of six degrees in odd signs. This arrangement is reverse in the case of even signs… One fourth, half, full, negligible and nil are the grades of results due to a planet in infant, youthful, adolescent, old and dead states."
> — BPHS 45.3–4

The mapping in odd signs is:

| Degrees | State (Sanskrit) | State (English) | Strength of result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0°–6° | Bala | Infant | one-quarter |
| 6°–12° | Kumara | Youthful | one-half |
| 12°–18° | Yuva | Adolescent | full |
| 18°–24° | Vridhdha | Old | negligible |
| 24°–30° | Mrita | Dead | nil |

In even signs the order reverses — 0°–6° is Mrita, 6°–12° is Vridhdha, and so on, ending with Bala at 24°–30°.

The reading is structural: a planet's *Baladi* state determines the proportion of its full effect that comes through. A planet exalted in dignity but in *Mrita Avastha* (the dead state) loses most of its delivery power. The DashaflowView column "Strength Factor" is this proportion.

## The three sign-based states — Awakening, Dreaming, Sleeping

Parasara overlays a second scheme based on the planet's sign-relationship. *BPHS* 45.5–6:

> "If a planet is in its own sign or in exaltation it is said to be in a state of awakening (or alertness). In the sign of a friend or of a neutral it is in dreaming state while in enemy's sign or in debilitation it is in a state of sleeping… According to a planet being in Awakening, Dreaming or Sleeping states, the results due to it will be full, medium or nil."
> — BPHS 45.5–6

These states (called *Jagrat*, *Swapna*, and *Sushupti*) qualify the *Baladi* reading further: a planet awake in a friendly sign but in an old state still loses most of its delivery; a planet awake in its own sign and in an adolescent state delivers in full force.

## The nine other states

*BPHS* 45.7–13 names a third scheme of nine more granular states based on combinations of placement, conjunction, and aspect:

> "There are nine kinds of other states, viz. Deepta, Swastha, Pramudita, Santa, Deena, Vikala, Khala, and Kopa."
> — BPHS 45.7

| State | Condition |
|---|---|
| Deepta | In exaltation sign |
| Swastha | In own sign |
| Pramudita | In thick friend's sign |
| Santa | In friendly sign |
| Deena | In neutral's sign |
| Vikala | In company of a malefic |
| Khala | In an enemy's sign |
| Kopa | Eclipsed by the Sun (combust) |

These states are not separately rendered in the section but inform the broader interpretation of the planet alongside the *Baladi* reading.

## How the section is read

Each row in the table shows one planet, the *Baladi* state it currently occupies, the degree it sits at within its sign, the strength factor (the fraction of full effect it delivers), and a short description of the state. The colour coding marks the state quality: infant (yellow), youthful (amber), adolescent (emerald — full strength), old (orange), dead (red — nil strength).

A planet's *avastha* is read alongside its dignity and its *shadbala* total: a planet can be exalted, meet its shadbala threshold, and still deliver only one-quarter of its result if it is in *Bala Avastha*. The three readings together describe how strong a planet is, how willing it is to act, and how much of that willingness reaches the chart.
