## Source verse

> The Yog becomes very strong for beneficial effects, if in the Antardasa of Moon in the Dasa of Mercury Moon is in a Kendra, or Trikona from Lagn, or, if Moon is in her exaltation, or in her own Rasi, associated with, or receiving a Drishti from Jupiter, or, if Moon is a Yog Karak herself. Then there will be marriage, birth of a son and gain of clothes and ornaments.
> In the circumstances, mentioned above, there will also be construction of a new house, availability of sweetish preparations, enjoyment of music, study of Shastras, journey to the South, gains of clothes from beyond the seas, gain of gems, like pearls etc.
> There will be physical distress, if Moon is in her debilitation, or in an enemy’s Rasi. If Moon is in a Kendra, Trikona, in the 3rd, or 11th from the Lord of the Dasa, there will be at the commencement of the Antardasa visits to sacred shrines, patience, enthusiasm and gains of wealth from foreign countries.
> Danger from the king, fire and thieves, defamation, or disgrace and loss of wealth on account of wife, destruction of agricultural lands and cattle etc. will be the results, if Moon is weak and is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be physical distress, if Moon is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. There will be relief, prolongation of longevity and restoration of comforts by the beneficence of Goddess Durga, if the Mantras of the Goddess are recited in the prescribed manner and clothes are given in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period of intellectual emotion. Favourable when the Moon is waxing and in a kendra or trikona — gain through women, learning, communication, family stability. Adverse when waning or in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth — mental agitation, miscommunication, illness of mother. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
