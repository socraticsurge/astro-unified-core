## Source verse

> Gain of jewels, like pearls etc., learning, increase in happiness and performance of pious deeds, success in the educational sphere, acquisition of name and fame, meeting with new kings, gain of wealth and happiness from wife, children and parents will be the effects in the Antardasa of Mercury in his own Dasa, if Mercury is placed in his exaltation Rasi, or is otherwise well placed.
> There will be loss of wealth and cattle, antagonism with kinsmen, diseases, like stomach pains, piety in discharging duties, as a government official, if Mercury is in his debilitation Rasi etc., or, if Mercury is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya, or, if Mercury is associated with malefics.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mercury antardasha within his own mahadasha as a period where intellect and communication peak. Favourable when Mercury is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — gain through learning, commerce, eloquence, scholarship. Adverse when debilitated or in a difficult house — disputes through speech, mental confusion, nervous afflictions. Marak risk if Mercury rules the second or seventh; worship of Vishnu is prescribed.
