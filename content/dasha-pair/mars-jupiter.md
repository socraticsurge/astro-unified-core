## Source verse

> Effects, like good reputation and renown, honours by government, increase in wealth and grains, happiness at home, gain of property, happiness from wife and children etc. will be realized in the Antardasa of Jupiter in the Dasa of Mars, if Jupiter is in Dharm, or Putr, in a Kendra, or in Labh, or in Dhana, or, if Jupiter is in his exalted, or own Navamsa.
> Acquisition of a house, land, well-being, gain of property, sound health, good reputation, gains of cattle, success in business, happiness to wife and children, reverence from government, gain of wealth etc. will be beneficial effects, if Jupiter is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa, or, if Jupiter is associated with the Lord of Dharm, Karm, or Bandhu, or Lagn, or, if Jupiter is in a benefic Navamsa etc.
> Danger from thieves, snakes, wrath of the king, bilious diseases, oppression by goblins (Prot), loss of servants and co-borns, will be evil effects, if Jupiter is in Ari, Randhra, or in Vyaya, or, if Jupiter is in his debilitation Rasi, or, if Jupiter is associated with, or receives a Drishti from malefics, or, if Jupiter is otherwise weak. There will be suffering from fever, or danger of premature death, if Jupiter is the Lord of Dhana. The remedial measure to be adopted to combat the above evil effects is recitation of Shiva Sahasranam.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Jupiter antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period that softens Mars's force with Jupiter's wisdom. Favourable when Jupiter is in a kendra or trikona — gain through dharmic action, religious work, marriage, progeny, recognition. Adverse when in the sixth or eighth from Mars — distress through ethical compromise, loss of position, religious doubts. Worship of Brihaspati is prescribed.
