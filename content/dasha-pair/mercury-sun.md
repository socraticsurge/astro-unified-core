## Source verse

> Effects, like dawn of fortune by the beneficence of the king, happiness from friends etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Sun in the Dasa of Mercury, if Sun is in his own, or in his exaltation Rasi, or in a Kendra, or Trikona, or in Dhana, or Labh, or in his exalted, or own Navamsa. There will be acquisition of land, if Sun receives a Drishti from Mars and comforts of good food and clothes, if such a Sun receives a Drishti from Lagn’s Lord.
> Fear, or danger from thieves, fire and weapons, bilious troubles, headaches, mental agony and separation from friends etc. will be the results, if Sun is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya from Lagn, or from the Lord of the Dasa and, if Sun is weak and associated with Saturn, Mars and Rahu.
> There will be fear of premature death, if Sun is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. Worship of Sun is the remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Sun antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period of dignified communication. Favourable when the Sun is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — recognition for scholarly work, royal favour, promotion. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Mercury — conflict with authority, ego-driven disputes, eye trouble. Mrityunjaya Japa is prescribed.
