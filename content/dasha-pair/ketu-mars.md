## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of land, village etc., increase in wealth and cattle, laying out of a new garden, gain of wealth by the beneficence of the king, will be derived in the Antardasa of Mars in the Dasa of Ketu, if Mars is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, if Mars is associated with, or, receives a Drishti from benefics. If Mars is related to Dharm’s, or Karm’s Lord, there will definitely be gain of land and enjoyment.
> There will be recognition from the king, great popularity and reputation and happiness from children and friends, if Mars is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in the 3rd, or 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be fear of death/disaster during a foreign journey, diabetes, unnecessary troubles, danger from thieves and the king and quarrels, if Mars is in the 8th, 12th, or 2nd from the Lord of the Dasa. In the above circumstances amidst evil effects there will be some auspicious effects also.
> High fever, danger from poison, distress to wife, mental agony and fear of premature death will be the results, if Mars is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. By the beneficence of Mars there will be enjoyment and gain of property, if, as a remedial measure, a bull is given in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mars antardasha within Ketu's mahadasha as a period of high accident risk — both planets are sharp. Favourable when Mars is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — sudden victory, breakthrough, release from old conflict. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth — body injury, surgery, blood disorders, conflict. Worship of Subrahmanya and Ganesha is prescribed.
