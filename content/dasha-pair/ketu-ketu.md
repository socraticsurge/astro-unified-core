## Source verse

> Effects, like happiness from wife and children, recognition from the king, but mental agony, gain of land, village etc. will be derived in the Antardasa of Ketu in his own Dasa, if Ketu is in a Kendra, or Trikona, or, if Ketu is related to Dharm’s, Karm’s, or Bandhu’s Lord.
> Heart disease, defamation, destruction of wealth and cattle, distress to wife and children, instability of mind etc. will we be the results, if Ketu is in his debilitation Rasi and, if Ketu is in Randhra, or Vyaya along with a combust Grah.
> There will be danger from diseases, great distress and separation from kinsmen, if Ketu is related to Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord, or, if Ketu is in Dhana, or Yuvati. The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above evil effects are performance of Durga Saptashati Japa and Mrityunjaya Japa.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within his own mahadasha as a period of inward turn. Favourable when Ketu is in a trikona or with benefics — spiritual breakthrough, release from old burdens, contemplative insight. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth — illness, foreign troubles, loss of relatives, sudden conflict. Marak risk if Ketu's dispositor is the second or seventh lord; worship of Ganesha is prescribed.
