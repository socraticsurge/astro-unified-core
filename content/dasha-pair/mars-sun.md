## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of conveyances, gain of reputation, birth of a son, growth of wealth, amicable atmosphere in the family, sound health, potency, recognition by the the king, extraordinary profits in business and audience with the king etc. will be experienced in the Antardasa of Sun in the Dasa of Mars, if Sun is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or, if Sun is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, or in Labh along with Karm’s Lord and with Labh’s Lord.
> Distress to the body, agony, failure in ventures, possibilities of suffering from troubles in the forehead, fever, dysentery etc. will be the effects, if Sun is in the 6th, the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, or, if Sun is associated with malefics.
> There will be attacks of fever, danger from snakes and poison and distress to son, if Sun be the Lord of the 2nd, or the 7th. The remedial measure to gain good health and wealth is to perform worship of Sun in the prescribed manner.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Sun antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period of authority asserted through action. Favourable when the Sun is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in his own sign — promotion, royal favour, recognition for courage. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Mars — conflict with authority, displeasure of father, eye trouble. Mrityunjaya Japa is prescribed if applicable.
