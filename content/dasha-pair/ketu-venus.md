## Source verse

> Effects, like beneficence from the king, good fortune, gain of clothes etc., recovery of lost kingdom, comforts of conveyances etc., visits to sacred shrines and gain of lands and villages by the beneficence of the king, will be derived in the Antardasa of Venus in the Dasa of Ketu, if Venus is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or, if Venus is associated with Karm’s Lord in a Kendra, or Trikona and there will be dawn of fortune, if in such position he is associated with Dharm’s Lord also.
> Sound health, well-being in the family and gains of good food and conveyances etc. will be the results, if Venus is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in the 3rd, or 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be quarrels without any cause, loss of wealth, distress to cattle, if Venus is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. If Venus is in his debilitation Rasi, or, if Venus is associated with a debilitated Grah, or, if Venus is in Ari, or Randhra, there will be quarrels with kinsmen, headaches, eye troubles, heart disease, defamation, loss of wealth and distress to cattle and wife.
> Physical distress and mental agony will be caused, if Venus is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above evil effects are performance of Durga Path and giving a tawny-coloured cow, or female buffalo in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Venus antardasha within Ketu's mahadasha as the longest and often softest period in the Ketu cycle — Venus is Ketu's friend. Favourable when Venus is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in his own sign — gain of vehicles, ornaments, marriage, artistic recognition. Adverse when afflicted — distress through women, loss through indulgence, romantic confusion. Worship of Lakshmi is prescribed.
