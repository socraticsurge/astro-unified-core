## Source verse

> Effects, like reverence from the king, opulence and glory, happiness from wife and children, availability of good food, gain of reputation by performance of good deeds, increase in the number of children and grandchildren, comforts by the beneficence of the king, religious and charitable inclinations etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Moon in the Dasa of Jupiter, if Moon is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in Labh, or, if Moon is in her exaltation, or in her own Rasi and, if Moon is full and strong and in an auspicious Bhava from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be loss of wealth and kinsmen, wanderings in foreign lands, danger from the king, thieves, quarrels with coparceners, separation from a maternal uncle, distress to mother etc., if Moon is weak, or is associated with malefics, or, if Moon is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya, or, if Moon is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Physical distress will be experienced, if Moon is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above effects is Durga Saptashati Path.

— *BPHS Chapter 56* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within Jupiter's mahadasha as a period of emotional fulfilment and household stability. Favourable when the Moon is waxing and in a kendra or trikona — marriage, progeny, gain of property, peace. Adverse when waning or afflicted — emotional turbulence, family conflict, illness. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
