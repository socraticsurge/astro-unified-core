## Source verse

> Beneficence of the king, gain of wealth, little gains of land at the commencement of the Dasa and substantial later, birth of a son, conferment of authority by government, gain of cattle etc. will be the results in the Antardasa of Ketu in the Dasa of Mars, if Ketu is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, in Sahaj, or Labh, or, if Ketu is associated with, or receives a Drishti from benefics.
> Birth of a son, increase in reputation, beneficence of Goddess Lakshmi, gains of wealth from employees, attainment of the position of a Commander of an Army, friendship with the king, performance of oblations, gains of clothes and ornaments etc. will be the beneficial effects, if Ketu is a Yog Karak and is endowed with strength. (Ketu assumes the role of a Yog Karak, if he is yuti with a Yog Karak Grah (Lord of a Kendra and a Trikona)).
> Effects, like quarrels, tooth trouble, distress from thieves and tigers, fever, dysentery, leprosy and distress to wife and children etc. will be experienced, if Ketu is in the 6th, the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. If Ketu is in Dhana, or in Yuvati, there will be diseases, disgrace, agony and loss of wealth.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period of high accident risk. Favourable when Ketu is in a trikona or with benefics — sudden insight, release from old conflict, spiritual breakthrough. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Mars — body injury, surgery, loss of relatives, sudden conflict. Worship of Ganesha and Subrahmanya is prescribed.
