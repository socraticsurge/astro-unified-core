## Source verse

> Effects, like physical felicity, gain of wealth, beneficence of the king, celebration of auspicious functions, like marriage etc., at home, availability of sweetish preparations, increase in cattle wealth, attending discourses on Puranas etc., devotion to deities and the preceptor, interest in religion, charities etc., worship of Lord Shiva etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Jupiter in the Dasa of Mercury, if Jupiter is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in Labh, or, if Jupiter is in his exaltation, or in his own Rasi.
> Discord with king and kinsmen, danger from thieves etc., death of parents, disgrace, punishment from government, loss of wealth, danger from snakes and poison, fever, losses in agricultural production, loss of lands etc., will be the results, if Jupiter is in his debilitation Rasi, is combust, or is in Ari, Randhra, or in Vyaya, or, if Jupiter is associated with, or receives a Drishti from Saturn and Mars.
> There will be happiness from kinsmen and from one’s son, enthusiasm, increase in wealth and name and fame, giving grains etc. in charity, if Jupiter is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa and, if Jupiter is endowed with strength.
> Agony, anxiety, danger from diseases, antagonism with wife and kinsmen, wrath of the king, quarrels, loss of wealth, danger from Brahmins will be the results, if Jupiter is weak and, if Jupiter is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be physical distress, if Jupiter is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord, or, if Jupiter is in Dhana, or Yuvati. The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above evil effects are recitation of Shiva Sahasranam and giving a cow and gold in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Jupiter antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period of teaching and ethical scholarship. Favourable when Jupiter is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in his own sign — recognition for learning, religious work, gain through teaching. Adverse when in the sixth or eighth from Mercury — moral-intellectual conflict, religious doubt. Worship of Brihaspati is prescribed.
