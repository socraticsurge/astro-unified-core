## Source verse

> Auspicious effects, like availability of sweetish preparations, abnormal gains in profession and increase in cattle wealth, will be derived from the very commencement of the Antardasa of Ketu in the Dasa of Venus, if Ketu is in his exaltation, or in his own Rasi, or is related to a Yog Karak Grah, or, if Ketu is possessed of positional strength. (It is not laid down anywhere, in which Bhava Ketu does get positional strength).
> In the above circumstances there will be definite victory in war at the end of the Antardasa. Moderate results will be experienced in the middle portion of the Antardasa and sometimes there will also be the feeling of distress.
> There will be danger from snakes, thieves and wounds, loss of power of thinking, headache, agony, quarrels without any cause, or reason, diabetes, excessive expenditure, antagonism with wife and children, going away to foreign land, loss in ventures, if Ketu is in the 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, or, if Ketu is associated with a malefic.
> There will be physical distress, if Ketu is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above effects are Mrityunjaya Japa and giving a goat in charity. Remedial measures for appeasing Venus will also prove beneficial.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of romantic withdrawal or transformation. Favourable when Ketu is in a trikona or with benefics — spiritual maturation in relationships, release from old patterns, contemplative insight. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth — separation, illness, loss of partner, foreign troubles in romance. Worship of Ganesha is prescribed.
