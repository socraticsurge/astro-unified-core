## Source verse

> Effects, like recognition from the king, enthusiasm, well-being, enjoyments, acquisition of a house, lands etc., abnormal gains of food, clothes, conveyances, cattle etc., success in business, construction of reservoirs etc. and happiness to wife and children, will be derived in the Antardasa of Moon in the Dasa of Ketu, if Moon is in her exaltation, in her own Rasi, in a Kendra, Trikona, in Labh, or in Dhana. The beneficial results will be realized fully, if Moon is waxing.
> Unhappiness and mental agony, obstacles in ventures, separation from parents, losses in business, destruction of cattle etc. will be caused, if Moon is in her debilitation Rasi, or in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya.
> There will be the acquisition of a cow, or cows, land, agricultural lands, meeting kinsmen and the achievement of success through them, increase in cows milk and curd, if Moon is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa and, if Moon is endowed with strength. There will be auspicious results at the commencement of the Antardasa, cordial relations with the king in the middle portion of the Antardasa and danger from the king, foreign journey, or journeys to distant places at its end.
> Loss of wealth, anxiety, enmity with kinsmen and distress to brother, will be the results, if Moon is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. If Moon is Dhana’s, Yuvati’s, or Randhra’s Lord, there will be fear of premature death. The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above effects are recitation of Mantras of Moon and giving in charity things, connected with Moon.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within Ketu's mahadasha as a period of emotional withdrawal. Favourable when the Moon is waxing and in a kendra — gain through inward attention, family healing, contemplative experience. Adverse when waning or in the eighth or twelfth — depression, mental agitation, illness of mother, family conflict. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
