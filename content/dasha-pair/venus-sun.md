## Source verse

> There will be a period of agony, wrath of the king, quarrels with the coparceners etc. in the Antardasa of Sun in the Dasa of Venus, if Sun is in any Rasi, other than his exaltation, or debilitation Rasi. (This verse does not appear to be correctly worded, because Sun does produce good effects in a position, other than exaltation, or debilitation. The position is correctly stated in the Chowkambh version of this verse)
> Effects, like acquisition of a kingdom and wealth, happiness from wife and children, happiness from employer, meeting with friends, happiness from parents, marriage, name and fame, betterment of fortune, birth of a son etc., will be experienced, if Sun is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, in a Kendra, Trikona, in Dhana, or Labh, or in Kendra, Trikona, in the 2nd, or 11th from the Lord of he Dasa.
> Distress, agony, distress to members of the family, harsh language, distress to father, loss of kinsmen, wrath of the king, danger at home, many diseases, destruction of agricultural production etc. will be the results, if Sun is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya, or, if Sun is in his debilitation, or in an enemy’s Rasi.
> There will be evil influence of the Grahas, if Sun is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. Worship of Sun is the remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Sun antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of dignified pleasure. Favourable when the Sun is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — promotion, royal favour, recognition for artistic work. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Venus — conflict with authority, ego-romantic disputes, eye trouble. Mrityunjaya Japa is prescribed.
