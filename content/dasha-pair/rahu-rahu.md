## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of a kingdom, enthusiasm, cordial relations with the king, happiness from wife and children and increase in property, will be derived in the Antardasa of Rahu in the Dasa of Rahu, if Rahu is in Kark, Vrischik, Kanya, or Dhanu and is in Sahaj, Ari, Karm, or Labh, or is yuti with a Yog Karak Grah in his exaltation Rasi.
> There will be danger from thieves, distress from wounds, antagonism with government officials, destruction of kinsmen, distress to wife and children, if Rahu is in Randhra, or Vyaya, or be associated with malefics.
> If Rahu is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord, or is in Dhana, or Yuvati, there will be distress and diseases. To obtain relief from the above evil effects Rahu should be worshipped (by recitation of his Mantras) and by giving in charity things, connected with, or ruled by Rahu.

— *BPHS Chapter 55* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Rahu antardasha within his own mahadasha as a period whose character follows Rahu's natal placement. Favourable when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona with benefics — sudden gain, foreign success, recognition through unconventional means. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth — accidents, fear from snakes, displeasure of authority, sudden loss. Worship of Durga is the prescribed remedy.
