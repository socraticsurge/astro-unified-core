## Source verse

> Effects, like cordial relations with the king, increase in wealth and grains, some popularity/respect, some possibility of becoming head of a village etc., will be experienced in the Antardasa of Sun in the Dasa of Rahu, if Sun is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, in Labh, in a Kendra, or in a Trikona, or in his exalted, or own Navamsa.
> There will be good reputation and encouragement and assistance by government, journeys to foreign countries, acquisition of the sovereignty of the country, gains of elephants, horses, clothes, ornaments, fulfillment of ambitions, happiness to children etc., if Sun is associated with, or receives a Drishti from Lagn’s, Dhana’s, or Karm’s Lord.
> Fevers, dysentery, other diseases, quarrels, antagonism with the king, travels, danger from foes, thieves, fire etc. will be the results, if Sun is in his debilitation Rasi, or, if Sun is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Well-being in every way and recognition from kings in foreign countries will be the results, if Sun is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, in the 3rd, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be danger of critical illness, if Sun is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. Worship of Sun is the remedial measure, recommended to obtain relief from the above evil effects.

— *BPHS Chapter 55* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Sun antardasha within Rahu's mahadasha as a period where authority and the unconventional clash. Favourable when the Sun is exalted or in his own sign in a kendra — promotion through unusual means, foreign recognition. Adverse when afflicted — conflict with authority, displeasure of father, loss of position, eye trouble. Mrityunjaya Japa is prescribed.
