## Source verse

> During the Antardasa of Ketu in the Dasa of Rahu there will be journeys to foreign countries, danger from the king, rheumatic fever etc. and loss of cattle. If Ketu is yuti with Randhra’s Lord, there will be distress to the body and mental tension. Enjoyment, gain of wealth, recognition by the king, acquisition of gold etc. will be the results, if Ketu is associated with, or receives a Drishti from benefics.
> There will be Isht Siddhi, if Ketu is related to the Lord of Lagn. If he is associated with the Lord of Lagn, there will definitely be gain of wealth. There will also definitely be increase in the number of cattle, if Ketu is in a Kendra, or in a Trikona.
> Effects, like danger from thieves and snakes, distress from wounds, separation from parents, antagonistic relations with kinsmen, mental agony etc. will be derived, if Ketu is without strength in Randhra, or Vyaya. If Ketu is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord, there will be distress to the body. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is giving a goat in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 55* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Rahu's mahadasha as a period at the axis of the nodes — sudden, karmic, transformative. Favourable when both nodes are in trikonas or with benefics — spiritual breakthrough, sudden insight, release from old patterns. Adverse when afflicted — illness, accidents, foreign troubles, loss of relatives. Worship of Durga and Ganesha is prescribed.
