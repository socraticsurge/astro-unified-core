## Source verse

> Acquisition of horses, elephants and clothes, devotion to deities and preceptor, recitation of religious songs in praise of God, acquisition of a kingdom, extreme happiness and enjoyment and name and fame will be the beneficial results in the Antardasa of Moon in her own Dasa, if she is placed in her exaltation Rasi, her own Rasi, in a Kendra, or in a Trikona, or is associated with the Lord of Dharm, or Karm.
> Loss of wealth, loss of position, lethargy, agony, antagonism towards the king and ministers, distress to mother, imprisonment and loss of kinsmen will be the evil effects in her Antardasa, if Moon is in her debilitation Rasi, if Moon is associated with malefics, or, if Moon is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya. If Moon is the Lord of Dhana, or Yuvati, or is associated with Randhra’s, or with Vyaya’s Lord, there will be pains in the body and danger of premature death. The remedial measures are giving in charity of a tawny-coloured cow, or female buffalo.

— *BPHS Chapter 53* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within his own mahadasha as a period whose character follows the Moon's natal strength. Favourable when waxing, exalted, or in his own sign in a kendra or trikona — gain of wealth, comforts, progeny, royal favour. Adverse when waning or with malefics — mental agony, illness, family disputes. Marak risk if the Moon rules the second or seventh; worship of Durga is the classical remedy.
