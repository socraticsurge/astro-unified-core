## Source verse

> Effects, like gain of wealth, cattle etc. through Brahmins, celebrations in connection with the birth of a son, well-being, recognition from the king, acquisition of a kingdom, will be derived in the Antardasa of Venus in his own Dasa, if Venus is in a Kendra, Trikona, or in Labh and, if Venus is endowed with strength.
> Construction of a new house, availability of sweet preparations, happiness to wife and children, companionship with a friend, giving grains etc. in charity, beneficence of the king, gain of clothes, conveyances and ornaments, success in business, increase in the number of cattle, gain of garments by performing journeys in the western direction etc. will be the results, if Venus is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or, if Venus is in his exalted, or own Navamsa.
> There will be acquisition of a kingdom, enthusiasm, beneficence of the king, well-being in the family, increase in the number of wives, children and wealth etc., if Venus is associated with, or receives a Drishti from a benefic and is in a friendly Navamsa, in Sahaj, Ari, or Labh.
> Danger from thieves etc., antagonistic relations with government officials, destruction of friends and kinsmen, distress to wife and children may be expected, if Venus is associated with, or receives a Drishti from a malefic in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya.
> There will be fear of death, if Venus is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. Remedial measures to obtain relief from the above evil effects are Durga Path and giving a cow in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Venus antardasha within his own mahadasha as the most pleasurable period in the Vimshottari cycle. Favourable when Venus is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — marriage, gain of conveyance, ornaments, artistic success, romantic fulfilment. Adverse when debilitated or in a difficult house — loss through women, financial drain, romantic confusion. Marak risk if Venus rules the second or seventh; worship of Lakshmi is prescribed.
