## Source verse

> Effects, like inclination to perform religious rites, fulfillment of all ambitions through the help of the king and friends, gains of agricultural lands and happiness etc. will be derived in the Antardasa of Venus in the Dasa of Mercury, if Venus is in a Kendra, in Labh, in Putr, or in Dharm. There will be acquisition of a kingdom, gain of wealth and property, construction of a reservoir, readiness to give charities and to perform religious rites, extraordinary gain of wealth and gains in business, if Venus is in a Kendra, in the 5th, 9th, or 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Heart disease, defamation, fevers, dysentery, separation from kinsmen, physical distress and agony will result, if Venus is weak in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord, or the Dasa.
> There will be fear of premature death, if Venus is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is to recite Mantras of Goddess Durga.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Venus antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period of refined expression. Favourable when Venus is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in his own sign — gain through arts, communication, marriage, beautiful surroundings. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Mercury — disputes through speech, romantic miscommunication, loss through indulgence. Worship of Lakshmi is prescribed.
