## Source verse

> Effects, like recognition from government, gain of house, land etc., happiness from son, extraordinary profits in business, bathing in holy rivers, like Ganges and foreign journeys, will be the auspicious effects in the Antardasa of Rahu in the Dasa of Mars, if Rahu is in his Multrikon, in his exaltation Rasi, in a Kendra, in Labh, Putr, or Dharm and is associated with benefics.
> Danger from snakes, wounds, destruction of cattle, danger from animals, diseases, due to imbalance of bile and wind, imprisonment etc. will be the results, if Rahu is in Randhra, or Vyaya, or receives a Drishti from, or is associated with malefics. There will be loss of wealth, if Rahu is in Dhana and great danger of premature death, if he is in Yuvati. The remedial measure to be adopted to obtain relief from the above evil effects are Naga Puja, offering food to Brahmins and Mrityunjaya Japa. They will help in the prolongation of longevity.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Rahu antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period of unpredictable turbulence. Favourable when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona with benefics — sudden gain, foreign success, victory through unconventional means. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Mars — accidents, surgical interventions, conflict with authority, snake-related fear. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
