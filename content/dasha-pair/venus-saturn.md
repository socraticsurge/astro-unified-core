## Source verse

> Effects, like great enjoyments, visits of friends and kinsmen, recognition from the king, birth of a daughter, visits to holy places and sacred shrines, conferment of authority by the king, will be derived in the Antardasa of Saturn in the Dasa of Venus, if Saturn is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, in a Kendra, Trikona, or in his own Navamsa.
> There will be lethargy and more expenditure than income, if Saturn is in his debilitation Rasi. Many kinds of distresses and troubles at the commencement of the Antardasa, like stress to parents, wife and children, going away to foreign lands, losses in profession, destruction of cattle etc., will be the results, if Saturn is in Randhra, or Labh, or Vyaya, or, if Saturn is in the 8th, 11th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. There will be physical distress, if Saturn is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord.
> The remedial measures to obtain relief from the above evil effects, are Havan with sesame seeds (Til), Mrityunjaya Japa, Durga Saptashati Path (by oneself, or through a Brahmin).

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Saturn antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of slow comfort. Favourable when Saturn is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in a friendly sign — gain through long-term partnerships, real estate, agricultural success. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Venus — distress through ageing partners, financial constraint, prolonged romantic struggle. Worship of Lakshmi and Saturn is prescribed.
