## Source verse

> The effects, like gains of wealth, beneficence of the king, performance of pious deeds and fulfillment of all ambitions, will be derived in the Antardasa of Sun in the Dasa Ketu, if Sun is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or, if Sun is associated with, or receives a Drishti from a benefic in a Kendra, Trikona, or in Labh.
> Danger from the king, separation from parents, journeys to foreign lands, distress from thieves, snakes and poison, punishment by government, antagonism with the friends, sorrows, danger from fever etc. will be the results, if Sun is associated with a malefic, or malefics in Randhra, or in Vyaya.
> There will be physical fitness, gain of wealth, or the birth of a son, success in performance of pious deeds, headship of a small village etc., if Sun is in a Kendra, Trikona, in the 2nd, or 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Obstacles in availability of food, fears and loss of wealth and cattle will be the results, if Sun is associated with evil Grahas in the 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. There will be distress at the commencement of the Antardasa with some mitigation at its end. There will be fear of premature death, if Sun is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects and to regain comforts by the beneficence of Sun is to give a cow and gold in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Sun antardasha within Ketu's mahadasha as a period where authority is tested by inward forces. Favourable when the Sun is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra — promotion through detachment, recognition for service. Adverse when afflicted — conflict with father, loss of position, eye trouble, displeasure of authority. Mrityunjaya Japa is prescribed.
