## Source verse

> Effects, like recovery of the lost kingdom, acquisition of desired grains, clothes and property etc., reverence from one’s friend and the king and gain of wealth, recognition from the king, good reputation, gain of conveyances, association with an employer and with men of learning, industriousness in the study of Shastras, birth of a son, satisfaction, visits of close friends, happiness to parents and son etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Jupiter in the Dasa of Venus, if Jupiter is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or in a Kendra, or Trikona to Lagn, or to the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be danger from the king and from thieves, distress to oneself and to kinsmen, quarrels, mental agony, loss of position, going away to foreign lands and danger of many kinds of diseases, if Jupiter is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa and be associated with a malefic.
> There will be physical distress, if Jupiter is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is Mrityunjaya Japa.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Jupiter antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of comfortable expansion — both are benefics. Favourable when Jupiter is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in his own sign — marriage, progeny, religious recognition, gain of property. Adverse when in the sixth or eighth from Venus — religious-romantic clash, ethical compromise. Worship of Brihaspati is prescribed.
