## Source verse

> Effects, like dawn of fortune, birth of a son, gain of wealth through judgement of court, listening to stories from the Puranas, association with persons, competent in poetry etc., visits of close friends, happiness from employer, availability of sweetish preparations etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Mercury in the Dasa of Venus, if Mercury is in a Kendra, or Trikona, or in Labh (from Lagn, or from the Lord of the Dasa), or is in his exaltation, or in his own Rasi.
> If Mercury is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of Dasa, or, if Mercury is weak, or is associated with a malefic, there will be agony, loss of cattle, residence in other people’s houses and losses in business. There will be some good effects at the commencement, moderate in the middle portion and distress from fever etc. at the end of the Antardasa.
> There will be physical distress, if Mercury is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is the recitation of Vishnu Sahasranam.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mercury antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of refined communication. Favourable when Mercury is in a kendra or trikona — gain through arts, eloquence, romantic communication, scholarly recognition. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Venus — disputes through speech, romantic miscommunication, loss through writing. Worship of Vishnu is prescribed.
