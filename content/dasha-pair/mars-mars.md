## Source verse

> Effects, like gains of wealth by the beneficence of the king, beneficence of Goddess Lakshmi, recovery of a lost kingdom and of wealth, birth of a son, will arise in the Antardasa of Mars in his own Dasa, if he is in a Kendra, in Putr, in Dharm, in Labh, in Sahaj, or in Dhana, or be associated with the Lord of Lagn.
> Fulfillment of ambitions by the beneficence of the king and acquisition of a house, land, cow, buffalo etc. will be the effects, if Mars is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, or in his own Navamsa and is endowed with strength.
> Urinary troubles, wounds, danger from snakes and the king will be the results, if Mars is in Randhra, or Vyaya, or is associated with, or receives a Drishti from malefics.
> There will be mental agony and body pains, if Mars is the Lord of Dhana, or Yuvati. Lord Shiva will give relief by restoring health and providing gains of wealth and happiness, if the person concerned performs Rudr Japa and gives a red-coloured bull in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mars antardasha within his own mahadasha as a period whose intensity follows Mars's natal placement. Favourable when exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — gain of property, victory, command. Adverse when debilitated or with malefics — body injury, fever, blood disorders, conflict. Marak risk if Mars rules the second or seventh; worship of Subrahmanya is the prescribed remedy.
