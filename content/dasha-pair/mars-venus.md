## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of a kingdom, great enjoyment and comfort of luxuries, gain of elephants, horses, clothes etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Venus in the Dasa of Mars, if Venus is in a Kendra to Lagn, if Venus is in his exaltation, or in his own Rasi, or, if Venus is Lagn’s, Putr’s, or Dharm’s Lord. If Venus is related to Lagn’s Lord, there will be happiness to wife and children, opulence and glory and increased good fortune.
> Gain of property, celebrations on the birth of a son, gain of wealth from the employer, acquisition of a house, land, villages etc. by the beneficence of the sovereign, will be the results, if Venus is in the 5th, the 9th, the 11th, or the 2nd from the Lord of the Dasa. In the last part of the Dasa there will be functions of songs and dances and bathing in holy water. If Venus is connected with, or related to the Lord of Karm, there will be construction of wells, reservoirs etc. and performance of religious, charitable and pious deeds.
> There will be sorrows, physical distress, loss of wealth, danger from thieves and the king, dissensions in the family, distress to wife and children and destruction of cattle, if Venus be in the 6th, the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, or be associated with malefics.
> If Venus be the Lord of the 2nd, or the 7th, there will be pains in the body in his Antardasa. For regaining good health the remedial measure to be adopted is giving a cow, or female buffalo in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Venus antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period of physical pleasure and impetuous attachment. Favourable when Venus is in a kendra or trikona — gain of vehicles, marriage, romantic fulfilment, artistic success. Adverse when in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Mars — distress through women, loss of wealth, conflict in love. Worship of Lakshmi is prescribed.
