## Source verse

> Effects, like recognition from the king, increase in reputation, gain of wealth and grains, happiness from children and grandchildren, increase in the number of cows etc. will be experienced in the Antardasa of Saturn in the Dasa of Mars, if Saturn is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, in his Multrikon, in his exalted, or his own Navamsa, or, if Saturn is associated with the Lord of Lagn, or, if Saturn is associated with benefics. Results will generally fructify on Saturdays in the month of Saturn.
> Danger from Yavana kings (foreign dignitaries), loss of wealth, imprisonment, possibility of affliction with diseases, loss in agricultural production will result, if Saturn is in his debilitation Rasi, or in an enemy Rasi, or, if Saturn is in Randhra, or in Vyaya.
> Effects, like great danger, loss of life, wrath of king, mental agony, danger from thieves and fire, punishment by the king, loss of co-borns, dissensions amongst members of the family, loss of cattle, fear of death, distress to wife and children, imprisonment etc. will be felt, if Saturn is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord and is associated with malefics.
> There will be journeys to foreign lands, loss of reputation, violent actions, loss from sale of agricultural lands, loss of position, agony, defeat in battle, urinary troubles etc., if Saturn is in a Kendra, in the 11th, or in the 5th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Effects, like death, danger from the king and thieves, rheumatism, pains, danger from the enemy and members of the family, will be experienced, if Saturn is in the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa and is associated with malefics. There will be relief from the evil effects by the beneficence of Lord Shiva, if Mrityunjaya is performed in the prescribed manner.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Saturn antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period where action and constraint pull against each other. Favourable when Saturn is in a kendra or trikona, exalted, or in friendly sign — gradual victory, agricultural gain, recognition through perseverance. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Mars — illness, debt, conflict with elders, prolonged effort with limited return. Sade Sati effects intensify.
