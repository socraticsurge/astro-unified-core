## Source verse

> Body pains, mental agony, loss of wealth, danger from the king, quarrels with the kinsmen will be the effects of the Antardasa of Ketu in the Dasa of Sun. If Ketu is associated with the Lord of Lagn, there will be some happiness at the commencement, distress in the middle part and receipt of the news of death at the end of the Antardasa.
> Diseases of teethe, or cheeks, urinary troubles, loss of position, loss of friends and wealth, death of father, foreign journey and troubles from enemies will be the results, if Ketu is in the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Beneficial effects, like happiness from wife and children, satisfaction, increase of friends, gain of clothes etc. and renown will be derived, if Ketu is in Sahaj, Ari, Karm, or Labh.
> If Ketu is Lord of Dhana, or Yuvati (or is in any of those Bhavas), there will be danger of premature death. The remedial measures for obtaining relief from the evil effects are recitation of Mantras of Goddess Durga (Shat Chandi Path) and giving a goat in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 52* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Sun's mahadasha as a period of body pain, mental agony, loss, and quarrel with kin. Favourable beginning if Ketu is conjunct the lagna lord, but with distress in the middle and news of death at the end. Specific afflictions of teeth, urinary trouble, loss of position, and foreign troubles when Ketu falls in the eighth or twelfth from the Sun.
