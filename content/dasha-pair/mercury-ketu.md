## Source verse

> Effects, like physical fitness, little gain of wealth, affectionate relations with kinsmen, increase in cattle wealth, income from industries, success in the educational sphere, acquisition of name and fame, honours, audience with the king and joining a banquet with him, comforts of clothes etc., will be experienced, if Ketu is associated with benefics in a Kendra, or Trikona, or, if Ketu is yuti with Lagn’s Lord, or with a Yog Karak. The same will be the results, if Ketu is in a Kendra, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Fall from a conveyance, distress to son, danger from the king, indulgence in sinful deeds, danger from scorpions etc., quarrels with the menials, sorrow, diseases and association with menials etc. will be the results, if Ketu is yuti with malefics in the 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be physical distress, if Ketu is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects, is giving a goat in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period of intellectual reorientation. Favourable when Ketu is in a trikona or with benefics — fresh insight, release from outdated thinking, breakthrough in scholarly work. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth — confusion, disputes, foreign troubles, loss of intellectual peers. Worship of Ganesha is prescribed.
