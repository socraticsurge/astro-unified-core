## Source verse

> Auspicious effects, like acquisition of land, gain of wealth and grains, acquisition of a house etc. will be derived in the Antardasa of Mars in the Dasa of Sun, if Mars is in his exaltation Rasi, in his own Rasi, in a Kendra, or in a Trikona. All-round gains, attainment of the position of a Commander of the Army, destruction of enemies, peace of mind, family comforts and increase in the number of co-borns will be the effects, if Mars is yuti with the Lagn’s Lord.
> Brutality, mental ailment, imprisonment, loss of kinsmen, disputes with brothers and failure in ventures will result, if Mars is in the 8th, or in the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, if Mars is associated with malefics, or, if Mars is without dignity and strength.
> Destruction of wealth by the displeasure of the king will be the effect, if Mars is in his debilitation Rasi, or be weak. Diseases of the mind and body will result, if Mars is the Lord of Dhana, or Yuvati Bhava. Recovery from ill health, increase in longevity and success in adventures are possible, if remedial measure, like recitation of Vedas, Japa and Vrashotsarg are performed in the prescribed manner.

— *BPHS Chapter 52* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mars antardasha within Sun's mahadasha as a period of acquisition and confrontation. Favourable when Mars is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona, or conjunct the lagna lord — land, wealth, victory over enemies. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from the Sun, or with malefics — disputes with brothers, mental ailment, failure. Marak risk if Mars rules the second or seventh.
