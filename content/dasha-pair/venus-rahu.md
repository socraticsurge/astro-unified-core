## Source verse

> Effects, like great enjoyment, gain of wealth, visits of friends, successful journeys, gain of cattle and land etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Rahu in the Dasa of Venus, if Rahu is in a Kendra, or Trikona, or in Labh, or, if Rahu is in his exaltation, or in his own Rasi, or is associated with, or receives a Drishti from benefics.
> Enjoyments, destruction of enemy, enthusiasm and beneficence of the king will be the results, if Rahu is in Sahaj, or Ari, or Karm, or Labh. Good effects will be experienced up to 5 months from the commencement of the Antardasa, but at the end of the Dasa there will be danger from fevers and indigestion.
> In the above circumstances, except for obstacles in ventures and journeys and worries, there will be all enjoyment, like those of a king. Journeys to foreign lands will bring success and the person will return safely to his homeland. There will also be blessings from Brahmins and auspicious results consequent to visits to holy places.
> There will be inauspicious effects on oneself and one’s parents and antagonism with people, if Rahu be associated with a malefic in the 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. Physical distress will be caused, if Rahu is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is Mrityunjaya Japa.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Rahu antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of unconventional pleasure and foreign romance. Favourable when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona with benefics — foreign marriage, sudden artistic success, gain through unusual means. Adverse when afflicted — distress through women, loss through indulgence, foreign-romantic deception. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
