## Source verse

> Effects, like increase of wealth and gain of wealth, grains, cattle, lands, village from a Yavan king, will be derived in the Antardasa of Rahu in the Dasa of Ketu, if Rahu is in his exaltation, his own, in a friends Rasi, or in a Kendra, or Trikona, or in Labh, or Sahaj, or Dhana. There will be some trouble at the commencement of the Dasa, but all will be well later.
> Frequent urination, weakness in the body, cold fever, danger from thieves, intermittent fever, opprobrium, quarrels, diabetes, pain in stomach will be the results, if Rahu is associated with a malefic in Randhra, or in Vyaya. There will be distress and danger, if Rahu is in Dhana, or in Yuvati. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is Durga Saptashati Path.

— *BPHS Chapter 59* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Rahu antardasha within Ketu's mahadasha as a karmic axis period. Favourable when both nodes are in trikonas or with benefics — sudden insight, foreign breakthrough, transformation. Adverse when afflicted — illness, accidents, foreign troubles, loss of relatives, snake-related fear. Worship of Durga and Ganesha is prescribed.
