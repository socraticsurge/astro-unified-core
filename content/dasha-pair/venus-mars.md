## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of kingdom, property, clothes, ornaments, land and desired objects, will be derived in the Antardasa of Mars in the Dasa of Venus, if Mars is in a Kendra, or Trikona, or in Labh, or, if Mars is in his exaltation Rasi, or is in one of his own Rāśis, or is associated with the Lagn’s, Dharm’s, or Karm’s Lord.
> There will be fever from cold, diseases (like fever) to parents, loss of position, quarrels, antagonism with the king and government officials, extravagant expenditure etc., if Mars is in Ari, Randhra, or Vyaya, or, if Mars is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> Physical distress, losses in profession, loss of village, land etc. will be the results, if Mars is the Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord.

— *BPHS Chapter 60* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mars antardasha within Venus's mahadasha as a period of physical attachment. Favourable when Mars is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — gain through courage, romantic intensity, success in disputes. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Venus — conflict in romance, body injury, blood disorders, financial drain through impulse. Worship of Subrahmanya is prescribed.
