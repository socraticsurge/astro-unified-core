## Source verse

> Effects, like gain of wealth, enjoyment, happiness to wife and children, religious inclination etc., will arise in the Antardasa of Ketu in the Dasa of Moon, if Ketu is in a Kendra, in a Trikona, or Sahaj and is endowed with strength. There will be some loss of wealth at the commencement of the Antardasa. Later all will be well.
> Gain of wealth, cattle etc. will be the effects, if Ketu is in a Kendra, in the 9th, the 5th, or the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa and is equipped with strength. There will be loss of wealth at the end of the Antardasa.
> There will be obstacles in ventures, due to interference by enemies and quarrels, if Ketu be in the 8th, or the 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, or receives a Drishti from, or is associated with malefics. If Ketu is in Dhana, or in Yuvati, there will be danger of affliction of the body with diseases. Mrityunjaya Japa will give relief in all the evil effects and will ensure gain of wealth and property with the beneficence of Lord Shiva.

— *BPHS Chapter 53* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Ketu antardasha within Moon's mahadasha as a period of withdrawal and inward struggle. Favourable when Ketu is conjunct the dharma lord or in a trikona — spiritual insight, release from old burdens. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from the Moon — mental agony, foreign troubles, loss of mother or maternal kin, illness. Worship of Ganesha is prescribed.
