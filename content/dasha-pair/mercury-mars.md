## Source verse

> Effects, like well-being and enjoyments in the family by the beneficence of the king, increase in property, recovery of a lost kingdom etc., birth of a son, satisfaction, acquisition of cattle, conveyances and agricultural lands, happiness from wife etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Mars in the Dasa of Mercury, if Mars is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, in a Kendra, or Trikona, or, if Mars is asociated with Lagn’s Lord.
> Physical distress, mental agony, obstacles in industrial ventures, loss of wealth, gout, distress from wounds and danger from weapons and fever etc. will be the results, if Mars be associated with, or receives a Drishti from malefics in Randhra, or in Vyaya.
> There will be gain of wealth, physical felicity, birth of a son, good reputation, affectionate relations etc. with kinsmen etc., if Mars recieves a Drishti from benefics in a Kendra, Trikona, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> If Mars be associated with malefics in the 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa, there will be distress, danger from kinsmen, wrath of the king and fire, antagonism with the son, loss of position at the commencement of the Antardasa, enjoyments and gains of wealth in the middle portion of the Antardasa, danger from the king and loss of position at the end of the Antardasa.
> There will be fear of premature death, if Mars is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measures to be adopted to obtain relief from the above evil effects are Mrityunjaya Japa and giving a cow in charity.

— *BPHS Chapter 58* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Mars antardasha within Mercury's mahadasha as a period combining drive and intellect. Favourable when Mars is exalted, in his own sign, in a kendra or trikona — success in commerce, legal matters, sharp argumentation. Adverse when in the eighth or twelfth from Mercury — heated disputes, body injury, blood disorders, miscommunication leading to conflict. Worship of Subrahmanya is prescribed.
