## Source verse

> Effects, like acquisition of a kingdom, respect from the king, gains of wealth, sound health, gains of garments and ornaments, happiness from children, comforts of conveyances, increase in house and landed property etc., will be derived in the Antardasa of Moon in the Dasa of Rahu, if Moon is in his exaltation, in his own Rasi, in a Kendra, Trikona, or in Labh, or, if Moon is in a friendly Rasi, receiving a Drishti from benefics.
> Beneficence of the Goddess Lakshmi, all-round success, increase in wealth and grains, good reputation and worship of deities will be the results, if Moon is in the 5th, 9th, in a Kendra, or in the 11th from the Lord of the Dasa.
> There will be the creation of disturbances at home and in the agricultural activities by evil spirits, leopards and other wild animals, danger from thieves during journeys and stomach disorders, if Moon is bereft of strength in the 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lord of the Dasa. There will the possibility of premature death, if Moon is Dhana’s, or Vyaya’s Lord. The remedial measure to obtain relief from the above evil effects is to give in charity a white cow, or a female buffalo.

— *BPHS Chapter 55* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within Rahu's mahadasha as a period of mental disturbance and emotional turbulence — the Moon is most vulnerable to Rahu's afflicting nature. Favourable only when the Moon is well-placed in a kendra with benefics. Adverse when waning, afflicted, or in the eighth or twelfth — fear, depression, family conflict, illness of mother. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
