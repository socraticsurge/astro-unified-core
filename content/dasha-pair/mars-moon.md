## Source verse

> Acquisition of more kingdom, gain of perfumes, clothes, construction of reservoirs, shelters for cows etc., celebrations of auspicious functions, like marriage etc., happiness to wife and children, good relations with parents, acquisition of property by the beneficence of the sovereign, success in the desired projects will be the effects in the Antardasa of Moon in the Dasa of Mars, if Moon is in her exaltation Rasi, or in her own Rasi, or in a Kendra, or in Dharm, or in Bandhu, or in Karm, or in Lagn along with the Lords of those Bhavas. The good effects will be realized in full, if Moon is waxing. Waning Moon will reduce the impact of the effects to some extent.
> The effects, like death, distress to wife and children, loss of lands, wealth and cattle and danger of a war etc. will be experienced, if Moon is in his debilitation Rasi, or, if Moon is in his enemy Rasi, or, if Moon is in Ari, in Randhra, or in Vyaya from Lagn, or from the Lord of the Dasa. There will be the possibility of premature death, distress to the body and mental agony, if Moon is Dhana’s, or Yuvati’s Lord. The remedial measures to be adopted to obtain relief from the above evil effects, are recitation of Mantras of the Goddess Durga and the Goddess Lakshmi.

— *BPHS Chapter 54* (Hora Shastra). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

BPHS reads the Moon antardasha within Mars's mahadasha as a period where emotion meets force. Favourable when the Moon is waxing and in a kendra or trikona — gain through women, comforts, marriage, progeny. Adverse when waning or in the sixth, eighth, or twelfth from Mars — mental agitation, family conflict, illness of mother, blood-related illness. Worship of Durga is prescribed.
