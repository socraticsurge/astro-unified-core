## Source verse

> Aridra-Dissimulating in self-interest, proud, ungrateful, cruel and sinful.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Ardra-born as proud, ungrateful in manner, capable of acts of cruelty, sinful in tendency, and given to deceitful conduct. The nakshatra is ruled by Rudra — the storm principle — and the reading reflects the destructive-transformative quality. The qualities are general; benefic aspects on the moon notably soften the reading.
