## Source verse

> Chitta-Using various clothes and garlands, good looks and limbs.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Chitra-born as wearer of variegated clothes, attractive in appearance, with eyes and limbs that are well-shaped. The nakshatra is ruled by Tvashtar — the celestial artisan — and the temperament tends toward aesthetic sensibility and craftsmanship. The reading is general; specific qualities sharpen with the moon's pada.
