## Source verse

> Swati-Polite, merchant, kind hearted, not able to endure thirst, sweet tongued and generous.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Swati-born as self-controlled in conduct, of good qualities, charitable in habit, eloquent in speech, and skilful in commerce. The nakshatra is ruled by Vayu — the wind principle — and the temperament tends toward independence, mobility, and adaptable judgment. The reading is general.
