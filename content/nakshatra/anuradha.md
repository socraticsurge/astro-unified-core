## Source verse

> Anuradha-Master or chief, living in foreign countries, not able to bear hunger and fond of traveling.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Anuradha-born as wealthy, given to enjoyment, traveller of distant lands, susceptible to hunger, and unable to remain long in one place. The nakshatra is ruled by Mitra — the lord of friendship and oaths — and the temperament tends toward sociable mobility.
