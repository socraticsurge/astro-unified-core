## Source verse

> One born in Bharani will be determined, truthful, healthy, skilful and happy.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Bharani-born as determined, truthful, healthy, skilful, and happy. The nakshatra is ruled by Yama — the lord of dharma and time — and the temperament tends toward firmness and an instinct for justice. The reading is general; the qualities are intensified or qualified by the moon's pada and the broader chart.
