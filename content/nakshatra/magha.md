## Source verse

> Makha-Many servants and great wealth, enjoying, respector of elders and gods and very enterprising.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Magha-born as wealthy, given to enjoyment, devoted to elders and ancestors, observant of religious obligations, of regal bearing, and respected in society. The nakshatra is ruled by the Pitris — the ancestral fathers — and the temperament tends toward lineage-consciousness and inherited authority.
