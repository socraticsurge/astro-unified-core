## Source verse

> Sravana-Rich surroundings, learned, good and liberal or liberal to wife, wealthy and renowned.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Shravana-born as wealthy, possessed of a generous wife, cultured in speech, learned, and deserving of fame. The nakshatra is ruled by Vishnu — the preserver — and the temperament tends toward listening, learning, and harmonising disparate concerns.
