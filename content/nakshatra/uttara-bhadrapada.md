## Source verse

> Uttarabhadra-Good and witty speaker in society and meetings, happy, many children and grandchildren, successful over enemies and charitable.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Uttara Bhadrapada-born as eloquent in speech, gifted with progeny, victorious over enemies, learned, and respected by society. The nakshatra is ruled by Ahir Budhnya — the serpent of the deep — and the temperament tends toward profound learning, restraint, and responsibility.
