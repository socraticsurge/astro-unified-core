## Source verse

> Hasta-Enterprising, intelligent or (shameless), drunkard, cruel and thievish.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Hasta-born as resolute in action, shameless in pursuit of goals, drunkard in tendency or excess, cruel under provocation, and a thief if afflicted. The nakshatra is ruled by Savitar — the solar generative principle — and the temperament tends toward skilful handwork; the negative qualities surface only when the moon is afflicted.
