## Source verse

> Jyeshta-Few friends, contented, charitable, very irritable.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Jyeshtha-born as one with few friends, given to anger, content with own efforts, righteous in conduct, and capable of considerable achievement through self-reliance. The nakshatra is ruled by Indra — the king of the gods — and the temperament reflects authority earned alone rather than inherited.
