## Source verse

> Aslesha-Dissimulator, clever in selfishness, sinful, ungrateful and a cheat.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Ashlesha-born as deceitful in manner, ungrateful, given to wickedness, prone to ingratitude, and of cunning intelligence. The nakshatra is ruled by the Nagas — the serpent powers — and the reading reflects the entwining, watchful quality. Benefic aspects on the moon notably soften the reading; the moon's pada within Ashlesha is a strong qualifier.
