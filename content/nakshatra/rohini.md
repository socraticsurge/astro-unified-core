## Source verse

> Rohini makes a man truthful, clean, following religious and moral principles, sweet in speech, fixed mind and handsome.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Rohini-born as truthful, clean in habit, observant of religious and moral principles, sweet in speech, and steady in resolve. The nakshatra is the moon's most beloved and is ruled by Brahma — the creative principle. The temperament inclines toward beauty, fertility, and constancy. Specific qualities sharpen with the moon's pada.
