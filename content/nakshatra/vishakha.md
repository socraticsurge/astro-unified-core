## Source verse

> Visakha-Jealous, avaricious, handsome, clever speaker and quarrelsome, or maker of money.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Vishakha-born as covetous in tendency, eloquent in speech, capable, sometimes dishonest in pursuit of ends, and given to disputes. The nakshatra is ruled by Indra and Agni jointly — the lords of strategy and fire — and the temperament reflects ambition with capacity for sharp competition.
