## Source verse

> Punarvasu-Religious endurance, happy, good, dull, sickly, thirsty and pleased with small gifts.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Punarvasu-born as pleasant in temperament, of pleasing qualities, prone to recurring illness, charitable, contented in disposition, and not particularly intelligent in worldly cleverness. The nakshatra is ruled by Aditi — the boundless mother — and the temperament tends toward generosity and renewal.
