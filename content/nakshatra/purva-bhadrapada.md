## Source verse

> Poorvabhadra-Sorrowful, loss of money through females, skilful and miserly.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Purva Bhadrapada-born as much-troubled in life, with troubles from women, given to gambling and similar attachments, and capable of strong commitments when calmed. The nakshatra is ruled by Aja Ekapada — the one-footed serpent — and the reading reflects volatility that asks for steady application.
