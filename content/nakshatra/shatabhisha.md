## Source verse

> Satabhisha-Plain and truthful, striken from sorrow through females, etc., killer of enemies, adventurous and irreconcilable.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Shatabhisha-born as truthful in speech, frank in manner, conqueror of enemies, fond of women, and given to indulgence in addictive pleasures. The nakshatra is ruled by Varuna — the lord of cosmic waters and oaths — and the temperament reflects depth and a tendency to extremes.
