## Source verse

> Mrigasira-Capricious, skilful, cowardly, good speaker, hopeful, rich and enjoying.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Mrigasira-born as capricious in manner, skilful, somewhat cowardly in confrontation, a good speaker, hopeful in disposition, rich, and given to enjoyment. The nakshatra is ruled by Soma — the lunar nectar — and the temperament tends toward seeking and refinement rather than aggression.
