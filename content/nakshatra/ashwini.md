## Source verse

> The person born In Aswini will be fond of decoration, handsome, popular, skilful and intelligent

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Ashwini-born as fond of decoration, handsome in appearance, popular among peers, skilful with the body, and intelligent. The nakshatra is ruled by the Ashwins — the celestial physicians — and lent to those whose temperament includes a quickness of healing instinct and decorative grace. The reading is general; specific qualities sharpen with the moon's pada and the rest of the chart.
