## Source verse

> Uttara-Popular, self-acquired property, enjoying and happy.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Uttara Phalguni-born as kind in conduct, modest in manner, learned, lover of arts and entertainment, fond of music, and respected for character. The nakshatra is ruled by Aryaman — the patron of contracts and friendships — and the temperament tends toward reliable companionship and gracious hospitality.
