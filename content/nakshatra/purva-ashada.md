## Source verse

> Purvashada-Good and pleasant wife, proud and a steady friend.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Purva Ashada-born as proud in temperament, with a beautiful wife, devoted to family, of firm friendship, and inclined to traditional values. The nakshatra is ruled by Apas — the waters — and the temperament tends toward the warmth of close attachment and the pull of inherited custom.
