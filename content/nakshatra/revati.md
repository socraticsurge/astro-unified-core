## Source verse

> Revati-Well- developed organs, popular, courageous, clean and wealthy. developed organs, popular, courageous, clean and wealthy.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Revati-born as well-built in body, with high regard for self and conduct, given to charity, and possessed of considerable wealth. The nakshatra is ruled by Pushan — the protector and nourisher — and the temperament tends toward gentle affluence and concern for others' wellbeing.
