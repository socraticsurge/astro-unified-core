## Source verse

> Pushya-Control over passions, popular, learned, rich and charitable.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Pushya-born as calm of temperament, given to virtuous behaviour, of even disposition, charitable, learned in spiritual matters, and inclined to dharmic pursuits. The nakshatra is ruled by Brihaspati — the priest of the gods — and is treated by classical authority as the most auspicious of all twenty-seven for general activity.
