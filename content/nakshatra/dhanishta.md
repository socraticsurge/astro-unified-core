## Source verse

> Dhanishta-Liberal, rich, courageous, fond of music and money.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Dhanishta-born as charitable, wealthy, courageous, an enjoyer of musical entertainment, and discontented with given conditions. The nakshatra is ruled by the eight Vasus — the elemental forces — and the temperament tends toward acquisitive musicality with restless ambition.
