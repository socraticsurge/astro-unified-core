## Source verse

> Moola-Proud, rich happy, good, steady and enjoying.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Moola-born as proud in bearing, wealthy, fond of comforts, with a sweet disposition, and inclined toward philosophical inquiry. The nakshatra is ruled by Nirriti — the goddess of dissolution — and is treated in the tradition as a sensitive nakshatra requiring specific remedial measures (see Gandanta) when the moon falls in the early degrees.
