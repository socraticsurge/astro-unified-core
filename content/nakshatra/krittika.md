## Source verse

> The person born in Krittika will be a voracious enter, fond of other’s wives, attractive and renowned

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Krittika-born as a voracious eater, attractive, renowned, and inclined toward the company of others' wives. The nakshatra is ruled by Agni — the fire principle — and brings sharpness of perception with appetite that can become indiscretion. The reading is general; classical commentators read the moon's pada within Krittika as a strong qualifier.
