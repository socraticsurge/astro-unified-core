## Source verse

> Pubba-Sweet speech, liberal, handsome, fond of traveling and royal servant.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Purva Phalguni-born as charitable, given to enjoyment, devoted to dharma, attractive in appearance, eloquent in speech, and inclined toward the arts. The nakshatra is ruled by Bhaga — the lord of fortune and pleasure — and the temperament tends toward generosity with means and refinement of taste.
