## Source verse

> Uttarashada-Polite, knowing, virtuous, many friends, grateful and popular.

— *Brihat Jataka, Chapter 16* (effects of the Moon in the nakshatras). Adapted from the Maitreya database (GPL v2.0+); see content/CREDITS.md.

## Rendering

Brihat Jataka reads the Uttara Ashada-born as obedient, given to gratitude, possessed of many friends, victorious over enemies, and a leader in conduct. The nakshatra is ruled by the Vishvedevas — the universal gods — and the temperament tends toward integrative leadership and steady reliability.
